Within two years, I’ll become an AE, and then a lot of things will be sorted out.

But yeah, along with my FE job, I’ll keep studying because I know I can do better. I’ll definitely try to crack interviews with good MNCs and get a better package. And once that happens, most of the things will definitely start getting sorted.

I want you to choose me only if you genuinely believe that what we have, and what I’m trying to build, is worth more to you than simply having someone who already has everything.

I may not be able to promise you a perfect life from day one, but I can promise you that one day, everything will fall perfectly into place if you’re with me. There are many beautiful people in this world, but there is only one you. And that's what makes you so special to me. The more I got to know you, the more I realized that my favorite place has never been a city, a destination, or a memory it's simply wherever you are.

So here's my heart, on paper, in ink that won't fade: thank you for being my favorite person. Thank you for being patient with me while I worked up the courage to ask you something important which, if you keep scrolling, you'll find waiting for you.

Forever yours,
